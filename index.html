Our Story

Ember & Roast was born in 2012 from a tiny garage roaster and an obsession with great coffee. We source our beans directly from small farms in Ethiopia, Colombia, and Guatemala — building real relationships with the people who grow them.

Every cup we pour carries that journey from soil to steam. We believe coffee should be an experience, not just a habit.
